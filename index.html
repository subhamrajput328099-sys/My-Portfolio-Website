How I run campaigns

  1. 1
    Test small

    New products and creatives start at ₹500 a day.

  2. 2
    Read the data

    Judge by conversions and cost per conversion, not clicks.

  3. 3
    Scale the winners

    Move budget to what converts, with separate scale campaigns.

  4. 4
    Report by product

    Spend, conversions and cost per conversion for every product.
